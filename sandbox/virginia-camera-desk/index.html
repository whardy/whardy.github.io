About the camera data

Camera locations and feeds are provided by VDOT / Virginia 511. Metro and regional groups are browsing areas, not official metropolitan boundaries. County and city filters help narrow the catalog. Only groups with cameras appear.

Snapshots refresh every 60 seconds while visible. “Fetched” records when your browser loaded the image, not when the camera captured it. Sources can return delayed images or offline notices. Locations are from the dated catalog above; reloading the page does not discover new cameras until the catalog is rebuilt.

Open a camera and choose “Watch live” to start video where available. Only the selected video plays. Closing it or hiding this tab stops playback. Availability depends on the source and your browser. Saved cameras stay in this browser.
