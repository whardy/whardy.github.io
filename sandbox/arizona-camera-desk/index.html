Sources & image freshness

Camera locations and images come from AZ511, Arizona’s official traveler-information service. This is an independent viewer. City and area labels follow the source catalog; they are not official metropolitan boundaries. Only areas with cameras appear. Counties are checked against Arizona State Land Department boundaries using the reported camera coordinates.

Snapshots refresh every 60 seconds while visible. “Fetched” records when your browser loaded the image, not when the camera captured it. Sources can return delayed images or offline notices. The dated catalog contains camera locations; reloading this page does not discover new cameras until the catalog is rebuilt.

This page displays snapshots without video controls. Official viewers may offer additional media. Saved cameras stay in this browser.
