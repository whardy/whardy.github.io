About the camera data

Camera locations come from TxDOT ITS and the City of Austin’s public-domain camera catalog. TxDOT snapshots are delivered through our image relay; Austin snapshots come directly from the city. Houston cameras link to the official district viewer because TranStar requires permission for image embedding. Metro and regional groups help you browse the catalog; they are not official metropolitan boundaries. Only groups with cameras appear. This is an independent viewer, not an official TxDOT service.

Snapshots refresh every 60 seconds while visible. “Fetched” records when your browser loaded the image, not when the camera captured it. Sources can return delayed images or offline notices. Locations are from the dated catalog above; reloading the page does not discover new cameras until the catalog is rebuilt.

This page displays snapshots, with no video controls or iOS pause overlay. Official viewers may offer additional media. Saved cameras stay in this browser.
