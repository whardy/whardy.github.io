About the camera data

Camera locations and snapshots are provided by PennDOT / 511PA and participating agencies. Metro and regional groups are county-based browsing areas, not official metropolitan boundaries. Missing weather-camera counties are matched to PennDOT county boundaries; border and unlocated cameras remain separately listed. Only groups with cameras appear.

Snapshots refresh every 60 seconds while visible. “Fetched” records when your browser loaded the image, not when the camera captured it. Sources can return delayed images or offline notices. Locations are from the dated catalog above; reloading the page does not discover new cameras until the catalog is rebuilt.

Video opens on 511PA, which limits viewers to eight simultaneous streams. This page does not start video streams. Saved cameras stay in this browser.
