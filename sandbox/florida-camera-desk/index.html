SOURCE NOTES

Know what you see.

Florida 511 supplies the camera list and imagery. Source service status, dated checks, browser retrieval time, and verified image capture time are different facts. Separate feeds are not synchronized.

Use the official camera viewer when an individual view is unavailable. Source and catalog audit explains provider coverage and limitations.
