Sources & image freshness

Camera locations come from the Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT). Images come from WSDOT and the partner sources listed in its catalog. This is an independent viewer. Areas are browsing groups based on camera coordinates and counties, not official metropolitan boundaries. County labels use WSDOT’s county boundaries. Only areas with cameras appear. Duplicate image URLs are merged while preserving route aliases. The catalog includes highway, local-road, ferry and airport views listed by WSDOT.

Snapshots refresh every 5 minutes while visible. “Fetched” records when your browser loaded the image, not when the camera captured it. Sources can return delayed images or offline notices. The dated catalog contains camera locations; reloading this page does not discover new cameras until the catalog is rebuilt.

WSDOT refreshes most source images about every five minutes. This page displays snapshots without video controls. Official viewers may offer additional media. Saved cameras stay in this browser.
