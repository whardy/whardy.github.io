About the camera data

Camera locations and public feeds come from Maryland CHART. Metro and regional groups help you browse; they are not official metropolitan boundaries. County and road filters narrow your view. Only groups with cameras appear.

Snapshots refresh every 60 seconds while visible. “Fetched” records when your browser loaded the image, not when the camera captured it. Sources can return delayed images or offline notices. Locations are from the dated catalog above; reloading the page does not discover new cameras until the catalog is rebuilt.

Open a camera to see the available viewing options. Feed availability depends on the source and your browser. Saved cameras stay in this browser. This independent viewer does not record or archive camera footage.
