About the camera data

This independent viewer uses 511NY’s public camera map. Feed owners include NYSDOT and NYC DOT. County and borough labels are assigned using New York State GIS boundaries; out-of-state and invalid coordinates are excluded. Areas are browsing groups, not official metropolitan boundaries.

Only visible still images refresh, every 60 seconds while this tab is active. “Fetched” records when your browser requested a picture, not when the camera captured it. Video loads only after you select Watch live. Reloading this page does not discover newly added cameras until the dated catalog is rebuilt.

Saved cameras stay in your browser. This page does not record or archive images or video.
