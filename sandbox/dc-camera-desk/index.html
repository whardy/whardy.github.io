About the camera data

Browse public District of Columbia traffic-camera locations by quadrant, ward and road. Only groups with cameras appear. Camera feeds and locations are supplied by DDOT through TrafficView. Quadrants and wards use official DC GIS boundaries. Dated availability checks are listed with the catalog.

Snapshots refresh every 60 seconds while visible. “Fetched” records when your browser loaded the image, not when the camera captured it. Sources can return delayed images or offline notices. Locations are from the dated catalog above; reloading the page does not discover new cameras until the catalog is rebuilt.

This page shows snapshots. Open TrafficView from a camera for its official live viewer. Saved cameras stay in this browser. This independent viewer does not record or archive camera footage.
